Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Coachr ("Coachr," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Coachr mobile application, website, and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your email address, a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text), your email-verification status, and your age group (13–17 or 18 and older) with the date and policy version of that declaration. We do not collect your date of birth.

Content you add. Team names, player names, positions and preferences, game details, sporting rules, saved lineups, and any league information you choose to publish. Only enter information you have the right to provide, and avoid unnecessary personal details.

Purchase information. Apple processes all payments. We receive transaction identifiers and subscription status to provide Pro access. We never receive your payment card details.

Support messages. Your email address and the contents of any message you send us.

Technical information. Limited request and error data (such as IP address, device and app information, and timestamps) used for security, abuse prevention, and reliability. Diagnostic logs exclude passwords and the contents of your requests.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and provide the Service.
  • To generate lineups and process the sporting rules you submit.
  • To manage subscriptions and verify purchases.
  • To send account emails, such as verification and password resets.
  • To show nonpersonalized ads to eligible free accounts.
  • To respond to support requests, moderate public content, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service, and only for the purposes listed:

  • Railway: hosting and database.
  • Resend: delivering account emails.
  • Apple: processing purchases and subscriptions.
  • OpenAI: processing sporting rules you submit (see section 4).
  • Google AdMob and its consent platform: serving nonpersonalized ads and collecting ad-consent choices. Google may process IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and consent choices.
  • Photon (Komoot/OpenStreetMap) and Zippopotam.us: looking up the city or postal code you type when searching for a league location. We do not access your device's GPS location.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users or others, to enforce our Terms of Use, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

4. AI rules processing

When you submit sporting rules, we send the rules text, sport, field and period settings, and related instructions to OpenAI to interpret the rules, write summaries, and build or repair a lineup engine. Submitting rules starts this processing. Your team roster is not sent to OpenAI for ordinary lineup generation.

Do not include player names, contact details, health information, or other personal information in rules text. A request that has already been sent cannot be recalled. If you prefer not to submit rules for AI processing, use existing approved rules instead.

5. Advertising

Free accounts may see nonpersonalized ads. We do not show personalized ads or use remarketing. Ads are not shown to teen accounts, or when consent or account status is unresolved. Where required by law, we ask for advertising consent through Google's consent platform, and you can change your ad privacy choices in Profile. Declining ads consent does not limit free lineup features.

6. Public content

If you publish a league, the approved league details and sporting rules become visible to other users. Private drafts stay private. If you report content, we keep the report, the reported content, and any moderation actions. Do not include sensitive personal information in public content.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You can delete your account at any time in Profile > Delete account. This removes your account and private data from our active systems. Shared league rules used by other coaches may remain with your personal attribution removed. Copies in backups and records we must keep by law are deleted on their normal schedule.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel it in your Apple subscription settings.

8. Children's privacy

Coachr is not directed to children under 13, and we do not allow anyone under 13 to create an account. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we will restrict it and delete its personal information. Teens aged 13–17 should use the Service with a parent's or guardian's involvement.

Coaches may enter information about players who are minors. Coaches are responsible for having appropriate permission to do so and should enter only what is needed. If you believe we hold information about a child under 13, contact support@coachrapp.io and we will review it and delete it where required.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update your age group, change ad privacy choices, and delete your account in the app. For any other request, email support@coachrapp.io. We will respond within the time required by law and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

We protect your information with password hashing, encrypted connections, and access controls. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International transfers

We and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries whose data protection laws may differ from yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app or by email.

13. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email support@coachrapp.io.
