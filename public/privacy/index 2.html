Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Coachr ("Coachr," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Coachr mobile application and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and a securely hashed version of your password. We may send a verification code to your email to confirm your account.

Content you create

The Service lets you enter and store content to manage your team, including team names, player names, player positions and preferences, game details, and generated lineups. You control this content and can edit or delete it within the app.

Subscription and purchase information

Coachr Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through in-app purchase. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive transaction identifiers and subscription status (for example, product purchased, and whether the subscription is active, expired, or cancelled) so we can unlock and maintain your Pro access.

Usage and diagnostic data

We may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, and basic diagnostic or error logs to keep the Service reliable and secure.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account and authenticate you.
  • To provide core features, including generating and saving lineups.
  • To process and maintain your Coachr Pro subscription.
  • To communicate with you about your account, security, and support.
  • To operate, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. AI-Assisted Lineup Generation

To generate lineup suggestions, relevant team and game context you provide (such as player names, positions, and game parameters) is sent to our AI processing provider, OpenAI, acting on our behalf to return a suggested lineup. This information is processed only to provide the feature and is not used by us or our provider to train AI models. Lineup suggestions are aids for your decision-making and are not guaranteed to be optimal or error-free.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate the Service:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf, including Apple (in-app purchases), our cloud hosting provider, OpenAI (lineup generation), and our email delivery provider (Resend) for verification and account emails.
  • Legal and safety reasons, such as complying with applicable law, a valid legal request, or protecting the rights, safety, and security of our users and the Service.
  • Business transfers, if Coachr is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and content for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account in the app, we remove your account, private team data, saved lineups, personal requests, and subscription records from our active database. Shared leagues and sporting rules may remain for other coaches, with your creator attribution and personal submission text removed.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and correction: You can view and edit your account and team content within the app.
  • Deletion: Open Profile > Delete account in Coachr, enter your current password, and confirm permanent deletion of your account and associated private data. You do not need to contact support.
  • Subscription management: You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple ID / App Store settings. Deleting your Coachr account does not cancel your Apple subscription or stop Apple billing. Cancel separately through Apple subscription settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information (such as access, portability, or objection). To exercise any right, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Coachr is intended for coaches and other adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Player names and related details are entered by the coach who operates the account. If you provide information about a minor, you represent that you are authorized to do so. If you believe a child has provided us information directly, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information, including password hashing and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Users

We may process and store information in countries other than where you live. Where we transfer information, we take steps to ensure it remains protected consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated Policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Policy or your information, contact us at support@coachrapp.io.
