Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Coachr ("Coachr," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Coachr mobile application and related services (the "Service"). The choices described below apply independently of your use of the Service.

1. Account and team information

Coachr is intended for people aged 13 and older and is rated 13+ on the App Store. We collect your email address and a password hash. We do not ask for your age or date of birth.

You can store team names, player names, positions and preferences, game details, sporting rules, and saved lineups. Enter only information you have the right to provide. A coach's authority to manage a team does not give unlimited permission to process a child's information. Avoid unnecessary personal information, especially in rules text.

2. Service providers and purposes

  • Railway and the application database: host account, team, rules, and service records so Coachr can operate.
  • Resend: receives the destination email and account verification or password-reset message to deliver account emails.
  • Apple: processes purchases. Coachr verifies transaction identifiers and subscription state to maintain access and purchase ownership. We do not receive payment card details.
  • OpenAI: processes submitted sporting rules as described below.
  • Google AdMob and its consent platform: support nonpersonalized ads and applicable advertising privacy choices. Ad services may process IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and consent information. Nonpersonalized ads do not mean that no device data is processed.
  • Photon (Komoot/OpenStreetMap) and Zippopotam.us: receive typed city or postal-code lookup queries and ordinary connection information when you search for a league location. This lookup does not request your device GPS position.

We also process limited request and error metadata for security and reliability. Local diagnostic tracing is optional and excludes credentials and request or response bodies. Infrastructure and provider logging and retention depend on their configuration; this policy does not promise a particular provider retention period, processing country, or training setting.

3. AI rules processing

When you submit rules for processing, Coachr sends your rules text, sport, field and period settings, and related instructions to OpenAI. The current flow does not ask for a separate AI-processing permission. Processing interprets rules, writes summaries, and builds or repairs a lineup engine. Requests may include generated code, reference engine code, synthetic test cases, and related validation failures. Personal details included in rules may be included in those requests. Ordinary lineup computation does not send your roster to OpenAI.

Use available approved rules if you do not want to submit new rules for AI processing. Avoid including player names, health information, contact details, or other personal information in submitted rules. A request already sent to a provider cannot be recalled. Advertising choices and public publication remain separate.

4. Advertising choices

Coachr requests nonpersonalized ads for free accounts. Unresolved consent or unknown subscription state withholds ad requests. Because we do not collect age, every ad request uses Google's teen age treatment, which disables personalized ads and remarketing and applies Google's teen ad-serving protections, for all users. Advertising consent is collected through Google's consent platform where required. Applicable ad privacy options are available in Profile. Denying consent or lack of ad availability does not prevent free lineup use.

Google's consent platform and Apple's tracking permission are distinct. The actual signed app's SDK settings determine tracking behavior; nonpersonalized ads alone do not establish an absence of tracking. Coachr's release disclosures must match the configured SDK and advertising messages.

5. Public leagues and safety

Publishing league information makes the approved public league fields and sporting rules available to other users. Private drafts and unpublished source text are not public merely because an engine is shared. Reports and moderation records may include the reporting account, reported content, reason, and review actions. Do not include sensitive personal information in public content. Public approval does not itself trigger new AI processing.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account and content records to provide the service. Use Profile > Delete account, enter your current password, and confirm deletion to remove your account and associated private data from the active application database. Shared sporting engines and safe league information used by other coaches may remain with personal attribution and private source text removed. Backup, infrastructure, provider, and legally required retention are separate and do not necessarily end immediately when active records are deleted.

Deleting your Coachr account does not cancel Apple billing. Manage or cancel separately in Apple subscription settings. A purchase has one live Coachr owner. After deletion, an eligible purchase may be reclaimed by a new account through verified Restore; deleting an account does not automatically transfer a purchase to another live account.

7. Teen accounts and children's information

Coachr is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we will restrict it and delete its personal information. Teens should review use of the service with a parent or guardian. Privacy, support, and account deletion remain available for restricted accounts.

Player information entered by a coach is different from a player's own account. If you believe Coachr holds information about a child under 13, contact support@coachrapp.io so we can review and take appropriate action, including deletion where required. Coaches should avoid entering under-13 personal information while that handling is being reviewed.

8. Your choices and security

You can correct your age declaration, access applicable ad privacy options, contact support, and delete your account from Profile whether you are free, Pro, or expired Pro. We use password hashing and encrypted connections, but no system is completely secure. We may disclose information when required by law or needed to protect safety and enforce terms. If you have a privacy-rights request, contact support.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the data flow or practices change. We will describe changes to the providers, purposes, or information used for AI processing. Contact support@coachrapp.io with questions.
